Our Story

Founded to bridge the gap between business strategy and technology.

Luminous X Technologies (Pty) Ltd is a South African technology solutions company incorporated on 03 May 2019 and registered with the Companies and Intellectual Property Commission (CIPC) under registration number 2019/197828/07.

Headquartered in the North West province, we are a 100% black-owned, youth-led enterprise committed to delivering world-class technology solutions to businesses and government institutions across South Africa.

We are powered by a team of passionate, highly skilled technology professionals who are driven by a singular purpose: to help organisations harness the transformative power of digital technology.

Mission & Vision

The two pillars that guide every decision we make and every project we deliver.

To empower businesses and government institutions by delivering reliable, innovative, and scalable technology solutions that simplify complexity, improve operational efficiency, and drive sustainable growth in the digital age.

Our Vision
