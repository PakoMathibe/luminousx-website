Common Questions

Things clients ask us.

Can we keep our existing numbers?

Yes. We port your existing landline and mobile numbers to the cloud — typically with no downtime during the switch. Your customers won't notice the change.

What internet speed do we need?

Roughly 100 kbps per concurrent call. A 10 Mbps link comfortably handles 50+ simultaneous calls plus normal data traffic. We'll measure during audit.

What if our internet goes down?

We recommend a backup LTE/5G failover for critical lines. Call routing can also fail over to mobile numbers automatically, so you never miss a customer call.

Can we integrate with Microsoft Teams?

Yes. We offer Teams Direct Routing and native 3CX Teams integration — you can make and receive calls from within Teams without switching apps.

How long does migration take?

A single-office migration typically runs 1–2 weeks. Multi-site or contact centre migrations run 3–6 weeks with phased cutovers.
