Common Questions

Things clients ask us.

Our data is a mess. Can you still help?

Yes — messy data is the norm. We start with a data audit, define the highest-value datasets, and work backwards to clean and consolidate.

How long until we see a dashboard?

First meaningful dashboard typically lands in 4–6 weeks, depending on data accessibility and stakeholder alignment.

Power BI or Tableau?

Both. Power BI integrates best with Microsoft stacks. Tableau is stronger for complex visualisation. We recommend after assessing your environment.

Do you build predictive models?

Yes — forecasting, churn prediction, anomaly detection, and other supervised/unsupervised models where the business case supports it.

Who owns the data and dashboards?

You do. Full ownership and access from day one, deployed in your own cloud tenant.
