Common Questions

Things clients ask us.

How long does an ERP implementation take?

Mid-market ERP implementation typically runs 4–9 months. Complex multi-entity rollouts can extend to 12+ months. Phased go-lives reduce risk.

How do you choose the right ERP?

We run a structured selection process — requirements, weighted scoring, vendor demos, and reference checks — so you choose based on fit, not marketing.

What about data migration?

Data migration is planned and tested as its own workstream. We migrate, verify, and reconcile before go-live so nothing surprises you afterward.

Can you work with our current systems?

Yes. Sometimes that means integrating rather than replacing. We'll recommend the option that delivers the best outcome at the lowest total cost.

How do you handle user adoption?

Change management, role-based training, and a hypercare period are built into every implementation. ERP success is 30% tech, 70% people.
