Common Questions

Things clients ask us.

Where do we start?

With a digital maturity assessment. It takes 2–3 weeks, maps your current state across eight dimensions, and produces a prioritised roadmap. Every engagement starts there.

How long does a transformation take?

Genuine transformation is a journey, not a project. Quick wins land in 3–6 months. Full modernisation of a mid-sized organisation typically runs 18–36 months in phases.

How do you measure success?

We define KPIs upfront — cost reduction, cycle time, customer satisfaction, revenue impact. Every milestone is measured against them, not against effort.

What about our people?

Change management is not optional. We build it into every phase — comms, training, and enablement — because transformation fails when people aren't ready.

Can we transform in phases?

Yes — and we strongly recommend it. Big-bang transformations have high failure rates. Phased, measurable waves are how we deliver.
