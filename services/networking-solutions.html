Overview

A network that disappears into the background.

A robust network is the foundation of modern business operations. Luminous X Technologies designs, installs, and manages enterprise LAN/WAN networks, structured cabling systems, wireless deployments, VPNs, and firewall configurations that deliver fast, secure, and reliable connectivity.

Whether you need a new network for a greenfield site or an upgrade to support growing bandwidth demands, our team delivers networking solutions built for performance and scalability.

Common Questions

Things clients ask us.

Do you handle structured cabling?

Yes. Cat6, Cat6a, and fibre — designed, installed, tested, and certified by our CIDB-registered team. Full test reports delivered on handover.

How long does a network rollout take?

A single-site LAN typically takes 1–2 weeks. Multi-site WAN and wireless rollouts run 4–12 weeks depending on size and cabling requirements.

Can you upgrade our existing network?

Yes. We assess what's working, what's aging, and what should be replaced. Upgrades are often phased to minimise disruption and cost.

Do you monitor networks after deployment?

Yes. Our managed networking service includes 24/7 monitoring, alerting, firmware management, and performance tuning under SLA.

What about Wi-Fi 6/6E?

We deploy Wi-Fi 6 and 6E where it delivers real value — higher density, lower latency, and better performance in congested environments.
