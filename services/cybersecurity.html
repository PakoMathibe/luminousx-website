Overview

Security that anticipates, not just reacts.

In an era of increasingly sophisticated cyber threats, protecting your organisation's data, systems, and reputation is paramount. Luminous X Technologies provides comprehensive cybersecurity solutions including threat detection, endpoint protection, network security, vulnerability assessments, POPIA compliance frameworks, and incident response.

We take a proactive, layered approach to security — ensuring your business is protected against threats before they materialise.

Common Questions

Things clients ask us.

Where do we start?

With a security posture assessment. It takes 1–2 weeks and produces a clear risk register with prioritised remediation steps.

Do you provide 24/7 monitoring?

Yes. Our SOC-as-a-Service monitors your environment around the clock, with escalation paths defined up front.

What about POPIA?

We provide compliance advisory, data mapping, consent frameworks, breach response plans, and ongoing audit support — all aligned to POPIA requirements.

Can you help after a breach?

Yes. Our incident response team can be engaged on retainer, ready to mobilise within hours of a suspected breach.

Do you offer penetration testing?

Yes — network, web application, and social engineering tests, delivered with structured reporting and re-testing.
