Overview

Infrastructure that just works — so your team doesn't have to think about it.

Your technology infrastructure is the backbone of your organisation. At Luminous X Technologies, we provide end-to-end supply, installation, configuration, and management of servers, workstations, networking equipment, peripherals, and smart devices. We design resilient infrastructure built for performance, scalability, and long-term reliability.

Whether you're setting up a new office, upgrading legacy hardware, or expanding your data centre capabilities, our team ensures your infrastructure is optimised for your specific operational needs.

Deliverables

What we deliver.

  • Server procurement, installation, and configuration (physical & virtual)
  • Workstation and laptop supply with standardised imaging
  • Networking hardware: switches, routers, access points, firewalls
  • Structured cabling and data centre setup
  • Peripherals: printers, scanners, monitors, docking stations
  • Smart devices: tablets, IoT sensors, digital signage
  • Hardware lifecycle management and asset tracking

Common Questions

Things clients ask us.

Do you supply and install, or just supply?

Both. We handle the full lifecycle — procurement, delivery, installation, configuration, testing, and handover with documentation.

Can you work with our existing hardware?

Yes. We regularly extend and optimise existing environments. We'll tell you honestly what's worth keeping and what should be replaced.

What warranty and support do you offer?

Manufacturer warranties (3–5 years typical) plus optional managed support covering monitoring, maintenance, and rapid replacement.

Do you handle structured cabling?

Yes. Cat6, Cat6a, and fibre — designed, installed, and certified by our CIDB-registered team.

How long does a typical deployment take?

A single-office rollout takes 1–2 weeks. Multi-site enterprise deployments run 4–12 weeks depending on scale and cabling requirements.
