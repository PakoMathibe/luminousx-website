Our Process

Assessment

We map your current infrastructure, workloads, and dependencies — and identify what should move, what should stay, and what should be retired.

Common Questions

Things clients ask us.

Which cloud provider is right for us?

It depends. AWS leads on breadth and maturity, Azure integrates best with Microsoft environments, and Google Cloud excels at data and analytics. We'll recommend the best fit after assessing your workloads, existing licenses, and team skills.

How long does a migration take?

A focused migration (a few workloads) can complete in 4–8 weeks. A full enterprise migration typically takes 4–9 months and happens in phased waves. We never do a big-bang cutover.

Will we save money?

Usually — but not immediately. The first 3–6 months often cost the same or slightly more as you migrate. Savings typically land from month 6 onward, once we've optimised instance types, storage tiers, and reserved capacity. Typical net savings land between 20–40%.

What about data sovereignty and POPIA?

We design for compliance from day one. AWS, Azure, and Google all have South African regions. We configure data residency, access controls, and audit logging to meet POPIA and sector-specific requirements.

Can you manage our cloud after migration?

Yes. Our Managed Cloud Services include 24/7 monitoring, patching, backup management, cost governance, and incident response — under a clear SLA.
