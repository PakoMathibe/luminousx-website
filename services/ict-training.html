Common Questions

Things clients ask us.

Where do you deliver training?

We deliver on-site at your premises, at our facilities, and online — whichever works best for your team and schedule. Multi-site programmes are delivered via hybrid formats.

Do your programmes count towards B-BBEE?

Yes. Our skills development programmes are aligned to the B-BBEE Skills Development element and can be structured to maximise your scorecard points — including learnerships and internships.

Do you run youth learnerships?

Yes. We run 12-month learnerships for unemployed youth in partnership with SETAs, providing both training and structured work experience. Candidates emerge job-ready.

Can you train on our custom software?

Yes. We build and deliver bespoke training on any system your team uses — including software we've developed for you. The training materials are yours to keep.

How do you measure training success?

Pre- and post-assessments, practical evaluations, and stakeholder feedback. We report on outcomes, not just attendance — so you know the investment paid off.
