Overview

Technology is only as effective as the people using it.

Luminous X Technologies provides corporate ICT training, digital skills development, and youth empowerment programmes that build capability and drive digital inclusion. We help your team get the most out of your technology investments and contribute to South Africa's digital skills development.

Our training programmes are practical, hands-on, and tailored to your specific needs — whether you're upskilling existing staff or developing new talent.

Common Questions

Things clients ask us.

Where do you deliver training?

We deliver on-site at your premises, at our facilities, and online — whichever works best for your team and schedule.

Do your programmes count towards B-BBEE?

Yes. Our skills development programmes are aligned to the B-BBEE Skills Development element and can be structured to maximise your scorecard points.

Do you run youth learnerships?

Yes. We run 12-month learnerships for unemployed youth in partnership with SETAs, providing both training and work experience.

Can you train on our custom software?

Yes. We build and deliver bespoke training on any system your team uses — including software we've developed for you.

How do you measure training success?

Pre- and post-assessments, practical evaluations, and stakeholder feedback. We report on outcomes, not just attendance.
