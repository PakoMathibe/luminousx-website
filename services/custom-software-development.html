Overview

Software that fits your business — not the other way around.

At Luminous X Technologies, we design and build bespoke enterprise applications, web systems, mobile apps, workflow automation tools, and API integrations that are precisely aligned to your business objectives. Common Questions

Things clients ask us.

How long does a typical project take?

Timeline depends on scope. A focused web app might ship in 8–12 weeks. A complex enterprise platform usually takes 4–8 months. We'll give you a realistic estimate during discovery — never inflated, never optimistic.

What's the typical investment?

Projects start at R150,000 for focused builds and scale based on complexity, integrations, and team size. We provide fixed-scope quotes after discovery so there are no surprises.

Do you work with existing codebases?

Absolutely. We regularly take over legacy systems, refactor them, and add new capability without a full rewrite — unless a rewrite genuinely makes sense. We'll tell you honestly which is the better path.

Who owns the code?

You do. Full IP transfer on final payment. We'll set up your repositories under your own GitHub/GitLab organization from day one.

What happens after launch?

Every project includes a structured hypercare period (typically 4 weeks). After that, we offer ongoing support, enhancements, and SLA-backed managed services.
